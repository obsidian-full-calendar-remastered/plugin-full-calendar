# Chrono Analyser

Chrono Analyser aims to make sense of your data, respecting your privacy and everything remains completely local in your device. No fancy tracking, no AI bullshit.

!!! info "Analysis Router"
    Use this module when you need quick interpretation of time data, not just calendar rendering. Start with the overview, then jump to configuration or troubleshooting depending on what is unclear.
